Yes Today

your cycle · your goals · your plan

A wellness planner for women that shapes each day around your cycle and your stage of life — cycle years, perimenopause or menopause — with a plan for food, movement and mind practices, written like a page in your own diary.

Your data stays on your phone.

support — luna.lifestyle.app@gmail.com

privacy policy

§ Getting started

How is my plan made?

From the questionnaire: your age, height, weight, goal, the activities you enjoy and how often you train, your diet and allergies, and the first day of your last period (or your stage of life if your periods have stopped). The app works out your daily calories, protein and training, then fills each day of your cycle with meals, sessions, a daily walk and two short mind practices. The Goal tab explains how your numbers were worked out.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in and no account. Everything is kept in the app on your phone.

§ Using your day

Can I swap a meal or a training session?

Yes. Open the item on the Day tab and choose another option. Swaps keep to your diet and allergies, and the day's totals update.

Where is today's note?

Tap the postcard at the top of the Day tab to open today's letter.

Is there a shopping list?

Yes — open it from the Day tab and choose how many days to shop for. It follows your swaps.

Can I use pounds, feet and fluid ounces?

Yes. Choose US units in the questionnaire or on the Info tab. Recipes stay in grams and millilitres.

§ Your cycle

My period started — what do I do?

Near the end of your cycle the Day tab shows buttons for this. Tap "Period started" and a new cycle begins from that day.

My period is late.

Tap "late" and the plan continues day by day until your period starts. If it's much later than usual, a note suggests when a pregnancy test or a visit to your doctor makes sense.

I changed my weight or goal — why didn't today change?

Today and past days stay as they were, so what you've ticked always matches what you saw. Your new plan starts tomorrow. If you change your diet or allergies, today's unticked meals update straight away.

§ Data and reminders

How do I turn reminders on or off?

On the Info tab, or in iOS Settings → Notifications → Yes Today.

Can I keep a record of a cycle?

Yes — on the Info tab, export a PDF report of a cycle and save or send it wherever you like.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your phone. Everything it stored is removed with it; we hold no copy. See the privacy policy.

I got a new phone — will my plan move over?

Only if you restore your new phone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one. There is no account to sign in to, so otherwise you'll start with a fresh plan.

§ Good to know

Is this medical advice?

No. Yes Today gives general wellness guidance. It is not a contraceptive or a way to plan or prevent pregnancy. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have a history of disordered eating, please talk to your doctor before changing how you eat or train.

Something isn't working.

Write to luna.lifestyle.app@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version (shown on the Info tab), and a screenshot if you can.
