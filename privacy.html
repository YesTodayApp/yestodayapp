Privacy Policy

Yes Today

Effective 28 October 2026

Your data stays on your phone.

no accounts · no analytics · no tracking · no ads

§ What Yes Today collects

Nothing. Yes Today has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party data collection. We do not collect, receive, store, share or sell any personal information — none of it ever reaches us.

§ What stays on your device

Everything you enter is stored only in the app's own storage on your phone:

  • your profile — age, height, weight, goal, activities, diet and food allergies, and life stage (cycle years, perimenopause or menopause);
  • your cycle — period start dates, cycle length and any late days you record;
  • your days — ticked items, swaps, sleep answers, notes, end-of-cycle answers and history.

We have no servers holding a copy and no way to see any of it.

§ Network connections

The app makes no network connections of its own. Fonts, drawings, recipes and all other content are built into the app, so Yes Today works fully offline.

The Sources page lists the studies and guidelines behind the app's health information. If you tap one, it opens in your web browser, and the website you visit is governed by its own privacy policy. Yes Today sends nothing with that tap.

§ Reminders

If you turn on daily reminders, iOS asks for your permission and the reminder is scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent to us or any notification service. You can turn reminders off in the app or in iOS Settings at any time.

§ Exporting a report

You can export a PDF summary of a cycle. It is created on your phone and opens the iOS share sheet; where it goes next — Files, Mail, a message — is your choice, and is handled by the app you choose, not by us.

§ Health information

Cycle dates, weight and wellbeing entries are sensitive by nature. They are treated like everything else above: kept on your device, under your control, invisible to us. Yes Today does not connect to Apple Health or any other health platform.

§ Backups and deleting your data

Deleting Yes Today from your phone deletes everything the app stored on it. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your phone to a computer, that backup — made by Apple, under your Apple account — may include the app's data until the backup is replaced or deleted. We have no access to those backups. There is nothing to request from us, because we hold nothing.

§ The App Store and TestFlight

Yes Today is distributed through Apple's App Store and, during testing, TestFlight. Apple may show us aggregated, anonymous statistics (such as download counts) and — only if you have opted in on your device — crash reports. This is collected by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and contains nothing you entered into Yes Today.

§ Age

Yes Today is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13. Since the app collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

§ Not medical advice

Yes Today offers general wellness guidance. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment and is not a contraceptive or a way to plan or prevent pregnancy. For medical concerns, please see a healthcare professional.

§ Changes to this policy

If our privacy practices ever change, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised before the change applies.

§ Contact
